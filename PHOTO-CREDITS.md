# Photo and illustration sources

Seven historical city/river context photographs are retained in their full compositions with photographer attribution and licence links. Their reviewed context use is not a general licence for architectural close-ups or bridge-focused crops. Photograph licences and rights in depicted works are separate.

The three original SVG graphics are generic concepts of riverbanks, walking directions and evening colours. They do not depict the actual bridge design, measured geography or current lighting, and are labelled as illustrations in all three languages.
